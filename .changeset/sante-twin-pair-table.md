**Migration.** Four establishments were published as a French-only record and an Arabic-only record, and are now one bilingual record each. The record keeps the id its French half shipped under, and the other id is retired for good: `01-eph-06` (the EPH of Adrar, merged into `01-eph-01`), `14-eph-07` (the EPH of Sougueur, into `14-eph-04`), `31-chu-04` (the CHU of Oran, into `31-chu-01`) and `31-chu-03` (the EHU of Oran, into `31-chu-02`). If you hold one of them, `data/retired-ids.json` carries a `migrations` entry naming the id it was merged into, both Ministry of Health post ids, and the full reading the pair rests on, so an old join key can be followed rather than only found missing. The record count falls from 661 to 657 and nothing was deleted.

**Why a hand-written pair table.** The registry publishes every establishment twice, once in French and once in Arabic, under two post ids, and the previous releases recovered those pairs from post adjacency plus a consonant-skeleton name check. Adjacency reaches at most two post ids, because only 42.9% of French posts have an Arabic post at id+1 and a wider window pairs the wrong establishments more often than the right ones. These four pairs, across three cities, sit **4, 10, 14 and 1284 post ids apart**, so the rule never sees them as candidates at all. In Oran the two halves of the CHU and the two of the EHU name nothing but the city beyond their class, so widening the window would have decided them on the registry's own class vocabulary, which every establishment in the registry carries.

They are asserted by hand in [`packages/sante/scripts/twin-pair-table.mjs`](https://github.com/yasserstudio/geoalgeria/blob/main/packages/sante/scripts/twin-pair-table.mjs). **Each row states what its evidence covers, in a `second_source` field, and the two levels are not the same claim** (Owner decision, 2026-10-22):

- `second_source: "both"`, a source which is not the registry names the establishment in **both scripts**, so it states the pair itself. The **EPH of Sougueur** has two independently mapped OpenStreetMap elements that do, [`node/5477990321`](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/5477990321) (`name:fr` "eph sougueur guermit Nasseur", `name:ar` "مستشفى سوقر قرميط ناصر") and [`node/2947184170`](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/2947184170). The **CHU of Oran** has [`relation/5484301`](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5484301) and Wikidata [Q7894776](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q7894776), which agree on both names independently.
- `second_source: "fr_only"`, the second source confirms the **French half only**, and the pair rests on that source plus two readings recorded with it on the row: the two registry titles are exact translations of each other, and the pair is the one candidate left in its wilaya and type group. The **EPH of Adrar** is one: [`way/393042095`](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/393042095) carries `official_name` "Établissement Public Hospitalier Ibn Sina Adrar" and no `name:ar`, and Wikidata [Q18785599](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q18785599) has a French label and no Arabic one at all. The **EHU of Oran** is the other: [`way/71816173`](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/71816173) names it in French at USTO, operated by the Ministry of Health, and its own `name:ar` is wrong upstream (it reads "public hospital establishment", not "university hospital establishment"), so that tag is **not** cited as evidence for the Arabic half. No Wikidata item for the EHU could be found carrying either label; the CHU's Q7894776 is a different establishment and is not cited on that row.

Each row also records the registry titles it was written about, and the build stops if the Ministry renames, moves or withdraws one of them, so a pair can never outlive the reading behind it. Google is not a source here, nor anywhere in this repository.

**Four cases are deliberately left as two records.** Skikda and Ouargla each publish an "Etablissement hospitalier" and an "Etablissement Public Hospitalier" of the city, in both languages, so neither is the one candidate left in its group, and nothing outside the registry names either one by its legal kind. Ouargla carries a second doubt on top of the first: only one general hospital of the city could be found in any source, so it is not even established that those four posts are two establishments rather than one entered under an older and a newer legal name. Pairing them would settle that by assumption. All four are listed in `unpaired_candidates` in the report, with the reading and the date behind the refusal.

**An Arabic locative particle was reading as a place's own name.** `بأدرار` and `بادرار`, "at Adrar", transliterate to `badrar`, which the commune-name exclusion did not hold, so the bare city name survived as if it told two establishments of Adrar apart. It had stamped the OpenStreetMap EPSP of Adrar, [`way/433204559`](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/433204559), onto the Arabic half-record of the EPH of Adrar. A token that is a commune or wilaya name reached through a leading `ب` or `ل` is now the place name it is, in Arabic titles only (in a Latin name a leading b or l is a letter of the name), and the EPH of Adrar publishes the reviewed Wikidata coordinate it was pinned to.

**The report also answers "is any Twin post still unpaired" on every build.** `half_record_candidates` crosses every half-record with every half-record of the other language in its own wilaya and type and prints the post gap between them: 63 candidates after these four merges, one of them inside the gap the rule reads, the Oran gynaecology EHS, which is refused on the names and already reported with its reason. A future registry refresh that leaves a new far-apart pair behind shows up as a row there rather than as two records nobody notices.

Also: bilingual records rise from 590 to 594, geocoded fall from 591 to 587 of a smaller total with the same 70 establishments unplaced, and exact points go from 133 to 132, the one lost being that wrong EPSP stamp. Every decision, the evidence level and reading for each hand-asserted pair, and every case left unpaired are in [`quality/sante-twin-recovery.json`](https://github.com/yasserstudio/geoalgeria/blob/main/quality/sante-twin-recovery.json), regenerated on every build so it cannot drift from the data. Both retirement sites, the twin merge and the repeat merge, now forward through one shared helper, so a retired id always resolves to a live record in a single hop even when its target is later absorbed by another merge.
