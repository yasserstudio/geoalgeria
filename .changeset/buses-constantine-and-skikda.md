Add the 25 ETUS-C Constantine and 6 ETUS Skikda Lines: 184 reviewed bus Lines across 16 Operators.

ETUS-C Constantine (wilaya 25) joins as the fifteenth Operator, with 25 numbered route identities and their Arabic and French termini transcribed from two Operator graphics supplied by the project owner: a route list and a schematic network map. The artwork is validation-only and is not redistributed, and the schematic is not treated as reusable geometry, so the new Lines are directory-only: no shape, no intermediate Stations, no service hours and no schedules, with the transcribed route colours kept as presentation evidence rather than a published colour standard.

ETUS Skikda (wilaya 21) joins as the sixteenth Operator, with the 6 numbered Lines of the Operator's own website page, read in a browser by the project owner because the site served an expired TLS certificate. Every Line starts at the city-centre square ساحة الشهداء, and the page publishes each Line's complete ordered Arabic stop sequence: those sequences are committed as Source evidence and `stops` carries their length (10, 9, 12, 16, 30 and 31), but the page gives no coordinates, so no Station and no shape ships. Only the shared origin has a sourced French name (Place des Martyrs, from OpenStreetMap); the other five termini stay Arabic rather than transliterated. The network-wide 06:00 to 19:00 service window, the 30-bus fleet and the six named served areas are recorded as Source evidence, not as per-Line fields, because the page never binds an area or a departure time to a Line number.

Shapes (76), Directions (128), Stations (1,603) and station memberships (2,685) are unchanged, and no existing record was modified.
