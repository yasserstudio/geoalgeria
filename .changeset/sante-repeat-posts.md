**Migration.** Seven establishments were published twice in the same language, under the same name, and are now one record each. The record stays on the registry entry that describes the establishment most fully, and the other id is retired for good: `05-epsp-01`, `05-epsp-13`, `08-eph-07`, `17-eph-11`, `22-eph-03`, `24-epsp-05`, `26-epsp-08`. If you hold one of them, `data/retired-ids.json` carries a `migrations` entry naming the id it was merged into and both Ministry of Health post ids, so an old join key can be followed rather than only found missing. The record count falls from 668 to 661 and nothing was deleted.

**The registry enters a few establishments twice in one language, which the twin-post rule cannot see.** The previous release merged the French/Arabic twin posts the Ministry of Health publishes for every facility. That rule pairs one language with the other, so it can never reach a facility entered twice in the language it already had: both copies ship as records and split the establishment's coordinate and anything joined to it. Of 1,258 registry posts, exactly 7 titles are published twice, and the conservative and the loose key of [`@geoalgeria/normalize`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@geoalgeria/normalize) agree on all 7. WordPress corroborates each one: the second entry carries a `-2` slug, which it only appends when the title collided exactly.

**A pair the registry names differently stays two records.** The gate is the whole title folded to the same key, which is stronger than the complete-match branch of the twin gate, and two further checks guard the placement. Two records that each name a commune outright, and name different ones, are two places whatever their titles agree on. Two records in different wilayas are two places too, unless exactly one of them is placed at all: a commune name repeats across Algeria, so an identical title alone is not evidence. Five candidates the weaker locality signal raised are kept apart on the names: the registry's own أريس 1 and أريس 2 in Batna, and the two Skikda and the two Ouargla hospitals, which it writes as "Etablissement Hospitalier" and "Etablissement Public Hospitalier" in French and as المؤسسة الإستشفائية and المؤسسة العمومية الإستشفائية in Arabic. Every decision and every pair kept apart, with both titles, both folded keys, both communes and both slugs, is in [`quality/sante-repeat-posts.json`](https://github.com/yasserstudio/geoalgeria/blob/main/quality/sante-repeat-posts.json), regenerated on every build so it cannot drift from the data.

**A Jijel hospital was published as a Djelfa record with no commune.** Post 4727 carries the Arabic name of the Jijel EPH and the wilaya tag of Djelfa, so it shipped as `17-eph-11`, in a wilaya its own name contradicts and with no commune, no coordinate and nothing for the OpenStreetMap pass to find a facility in. It is the one merge that crosses a wilaya, allowed because the Djelfa side is the unplaced one: it goes into `18-eph-02`, the Jijel EPH, which keeps its commune and its point.

**`refs.msp_repeat` is new**: the repeat post the record absorbed, alongside the primary `refs.msp` and the other-language `refs.msp_twin`. The carry-over key resolves through all three, so a record keeps the id it already had whether it gains a twin, loses one, or absorbs a repeat post. A record cites at most one repeat post, and a pair whose absorbed half would hand it a post in each language is refused rather than quietly dropping one.

Also: geocoded records move from 597 to 591 of a smaller total and 70 establishments remain unplaced, down from 71. One more record publishes an exact point, 133 rather than 132: the Ain Djasser EPSP shipped as two records standing on the same commune centroid, which the writer's shared-point rule has to demote to `approximate`, and merged it is one record on `node/3971311988`. Every other merged record changes only its `refs`.
