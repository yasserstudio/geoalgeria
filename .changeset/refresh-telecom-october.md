Refresh 5G coverage from the three operator maps, retrieved 2026-10-27: 3,598 records to 4,094.

Sources: Djezzy `djezzy5g.dz/map.html`, Mobilis `mobilis.dz/map/5g/data`, Ooredoo `ooredoo.dz` `/o/c/communes`. Previous pull 2026-09-08. mobilis.dz drops TCP from outside Algeria and the fetcher is all or nothing, so this pass ran over an Algerian connection the Owner supplied.

**Djezzy and Ooredoo did not change at all.** Both were re-pulled live and both payloads hash to exactly what was captured on 2026-09-08: `sources/telecom/manifest.json` keeps `9164e51a…` for Djezzy, 325,872 bytes and 1,001 rows, and `480b7625…` for Ooredoo, 166,332 bytes and 176 rows. Only their retrieval dates move. The whole diff is Mobilis.

**Mobilis: 496 sites added, nothing lost.** The raw capture goes 2,428 to 2,927 rows and the published set 2,421 to 2,917, after the same two filters as the previous pull: 7 out-of-Algeria points dropped and 3 exact duplicate rows skipped. Zero records removed, zero coordinates moved, zero renamed, zero changes of `geo_precision`, `geo_method`, wilaya or commune. That is a real property rather than an artefact of the id scheme: an id is `mobilis-{coordinate-hash}`, so a site that moved even slightly would appear as one removal plus one addition. Mobilis republished its 2,421 existing points byte for byte and appended. The additions are spread over 52 wilayas, led by Boumerdes 48, Oran 42, Constantine 37, Alger 25, Chlef 25, Blida 22, Mila 17, Bejaia 17, Djelfa 16 and Tipaza 13, so this is a national build-out and not one city. All 496 are `exact` / `operator_map`, which is why `exact` rises from 3,395 to 3,885 while `approximate` stands still at 185.

**109 existing records change one field only, `commune_ar`, and the operator's new Arabic labels are worse than its old ones.** No id, coordinate, wilaya, commune name or `name` changed on any record present in both pulls; the only field that moved is the operator's Arabic commune label, on 109 rows across 31 distinct communes. Scored against the Arabic commune names in `packages/dataset`, which is the official ONS registry rather than an opinion, 83 of those 109 matched the registry before and only 53 match now: 46 rows regressed and 16 improved. The regressions are mostly mechanical, a definite article prepended to a name that already carried one (`الميلية` to `ال الميلية`, `الكريمية` to `ال الكريمية`, `العريشة` to `ال العريشة`, `المرسى` to `ال مرسى`), a word duplicated (`فلفلة` to `فلفلة فلفلة`), or a different place entirely (`بئر الويدان` to `بئر العرش` for Bir El Ouiden, `بني عباس` to `بني بلعباس` for Beni Abbes). Some are real fixes (`بوزغن` to `بوزقن`, `ابن باديس` to `بن باديس`, `حمام بوحجر` to `حمام بوحجار`). They are published as the operator publishes them, which is what this package's contract says it does, and `commune_ar` is free text that nothing joins on: the id hashes the French commune, so no id moved. Whether to keep the operator's regressions or hold the previous labels where the registry disagrees is a decision this refresh does not take.

**Wilaya 62, Bir El Ater, appears for the first time, as a reconciliation and not a new region.** `wilayas_covered` goes 66 to 67. Its three records are Mobilis rows published under `source_wilaya_code` 12, Tebessa, the pre-2026 mother wilaya, whose commune is Bir El Ater, 1202. The existing reconciler moved them on an exact commune match plus polygon containment, and `source_wilaya_code` preserves what the operator said. Mobilis has not started publishing the 2026 wilayas; the repo does the translation. That reconciled set grows from 31 records to 40.

**Eleven of the 496 new sites declared a wilaya their own coordinate contradicted, and all eleven are triaged with evidence rather than blessed.** `research/_wilaya-containment/record-exceptions.json` is unchanged at 302: not one row was added to the residual. The decisions are in `quality/overrides/telecom.json`, each with the operator capture and the Journal officiel it rests on, and each published record carries its review receipt.

Five are a stale wilaya label over a point and a commune that agree, so the wilaya is corrected and the coordinates are kept. Three GOLEA sites move from 47 Ghardaia to 58 El Meniaa: GOLEA is the pre-2019 name of El Meniaa, canonical commune 5801, the operator's own Arabic label for the rows is المنيعة, and the same capture publishes one EL MENIAA row 1.6 km away under wilaya 58. One Chelalet El Adhaoura site moves from 26 Medea to 67 Ksar El Boukhari, where the 2026 reform put commune 2618; the reconciler missed it on spelling alone, operator `chellalat el adhaoura` against canonical `chelalet el adhaoura`, which is why three other 26 to 67 rows in this capture reconciled and this one did not. One Boudouaou site moves from 16 Alger to 35 Boumerdes, a plain operator error and no reform: commune 3502 has been in Boumerdes since 1984, the same capture publishes eight other BOUDOUAOU rows under 35, the nearest 180 m away, and this row alone says Alger.

Six are the opposite case, a point the operator's own commune and wilaya both contradict, so the coverage record is kept and the coordinates are withheld, as for the 18 reviewed Djezzy rows. The six RAS EL MA rows are one consecutive block of upstream ids, 34092 to 34097, all carrying longitude exactly -1.289 while their latitudes vary, and the same capture's four other RAS EL MA rows carry six-figure longitudes between -0.838 and -0.777. -1.289 is a frozen column in the feed, about 41 km west of commune 2223, which is why those points landed in 63 El Aricha. `geocoded_count` is therefore 4,070 of 4,094, 99.4%, and the accuracy review's `missing_coordinate` candidates for telecom go 18 to 24 with every other geometric verdict unchanged record for record.
