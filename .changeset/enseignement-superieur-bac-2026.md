Add the 11 higher-education institutions MESRS's BAC 2026 placement file holds and its university-network listing omits, and publish the current name of the 2 the listing still carries under an older one: 188 institutions from 177.

**A second MESRS publication, because the first one is unreachable.** www.mesrs.dz did not answer from the build machine on 27 and 28 October, so nothing here claims a new retrieval of the network listing: that source's `retrieved` stays 2026-08-03, the date of the committed listing captures, and `last_retrieved` in `refresh-schedule.json` stays with it, a package being only as fresh as its stalest source. `updated` (and so the dataset descriptor's `dateModified`) does move to 2026-10-28, because the data itself moved on that date. What was read instead is another of the ministry's own publications, *Moyennes minimales BAC 2026 (suite à la première phase)*, the 350-page file that names every establishment the ministry places new bacheliers into, each by the ministry's own establishment code. Committed from it is a rights-safe projection of its institution rows, `sources/enseignement-superieur/bac-2026.json`: 248 rows of establishment code and official French name, never the per-filière minimum averages and never the PDF, with the PDF's URL, SHA-256, byte count and page count recorded in the capture manifest.

**The projection is the authority for those names, not the seed.** `scripts/bac-2026.mjs` refuses any name in `scripts/seeds/bac-2026.json` that does not fold to the row the ministry published under that code, so the seed restores the French accents and capitalisation the all-caps source drops and can never introduce a name of its own. One misspelling in the ministry's file is corrected under a declared exception that quotes its row verbatim and is checked against the capture character for character, so the correction lapses the moment the ministry fixes it.

**11 new institutions**, ids `00178` to `00188`. Université des sciences médicales d'Alger; the Sidi Abdellah schools for mathematics, autonomous systems, nanosciences and nanotechnologies, and cyber security; the Annaba school of management sciences; the Adrar school of Saharan agriculture; the Khenchela school of forestry; the Saïda école normale supérieure, the thirteenth and the only one the package did not already carry; the Mohamed Lakhdar Hamina national institute of cinema at Koléa, under the Ministry of Culture; and the school of youth-work sciences and technologies in Algiers, the first establishment here under the Ministry of Youth, which `SupervisoryMinistry` now admits.

**2 renames, both keeping their id.** `00089` is now Ecole nationale supérieure des technologies et d'ingénierie (Annaba), from Ecole Supérieure des Technologies Industrielles de Annaba ex EP ST Annaba. `00093` is now Ecole nationale supérieure d'agriculture saharienne d'El Oued, from L'Ecole supérieure de l'agriculture saharienne - El Oued. A rename moves a name and never a join key: both records keep their website, which is what their id is pinned to, and the build refuses the rule if the listing has since changed that name itself.

**Ids no longer follow the sort position.** The generator numbered records 1..N in sort order, so inserting one institution mid-sequence would have renumbered every record after it and repointed every published join key. Ids are now carried on the stable key each record already had (its website host, or the Arabic name for the 48 establishments MESRS publishes without a site), through the shared `carryOverIds` contract, and a genuinely new record is minted above every id the package has ever used. All 177 published ids are unchanged and none is retired.

**Coordinates.** 5 of the 11 carry a real per-campus point, each the institution's own OpenStreetMap object, cited in the seed with its id, version and timestamp; none is a borrowed point. The four Sidi Abdellah schools were checked one by one: three have their own OSM building and the fourth has none, so it sits on its commune rather than on the shared tech-pole point that would have looked exact and proved nothing. The other 6 land on their commune's or wilaya's flagship centroid with the reason recorded per record. Across the package: 72 campus-precise from 67, 20 commune from 15, 96 wilaya from 95, and 0 of 188 records outside their declared wilaya.

**Arabic names are cited or absent.** 8 of the 11 carry one read from the institution's own site or from Wikidata, named per record; the 3 whose site did not answer and which have no Wikidata item ship `name_ar: null` rather than a translation.

**Provenance per record and per source.** The metadata now declares the placement file as a source of its own, `bac-2026`, with its own retrieval date and a `snapshot` naming the PDF's URL, SHA-256 and byte count, and every record says which publication its identity came from: `bac-2026` on the 11 the file supplied, `mesrs+bac-2026` on the 2 it renamed, `mesrs` on the rest. Neither source's `retrieved` is typed into the config: the build reads each from its own committed capture, so a listing pull can never leave the published date stale. `00187`'s Arabic name drops the trailing `(الجزائر)` it carried, which is Wikidata's country disambiguator on the label and not part of the name, and reads as the wrong place for an institute at Koléa in Tipaza.

This regeneration also reconciles the package's wilaya-centre placements to the flagship's reviewed centres, which had moved since the data was last built. Measured: **93 records move**, every one of them a `geo_method: wilaya` record borrowing its wilaya's centre. 70 move more than 500 m, 60 more than 1 km and 36 more than 3 km; 14 move 100 m or less, 3 of them under a metre; the median move is 1.4 km. The largest is **5,517 m**, shared by the 36 records placed on Algiers' centre, which left the sea east of the port for the Grande Poste (36.7325, 3.08722 to 36.776335, 3.058211). No campus-precise point and no commune-centre placement moved. One record changes its commune label, `00017` from Chetma to Biskra, without its coordinate moving.

**Type mix.** Grande école 111 (102), université 59 (58), école normale supérieure 13 (12), centre universitaire 5. By sector: 169 public (158) and 19 licensed private, 50 of the public ones under another ministry (48). Still 51 wilayas.
