**Migration.** Twenty-five establishments were published twice, once under their French name and once under their Arabic one, and are now one bilingual record each. The record keeps the older of the two ids and the other is retired for good: `01-eph-05`, `02-eph-08`, `02-eph-09`, `05-eph-13`, `05-eph-14`, `05-eph-15`, `07-epsp-07`, `07-epsp-08`, `08-ehs-04`, `08-eph-06`, `08-eph-08`, `08-eph-09`, `09-epsp-05`, `11-epsp-06`, `11-epsp-07`, `14-eph-06`, `16-ehs-13`, `16-ehs-14`, `16-ehs-15`, `17-eph-08`, `17-eph-09`, `17-eph-10`, `17-epsp-06`, `17-epsp-07`, `19-chu-02`. If you hold one of them, `data/retired-ids.json` now carries a `migrations` map that names the id it was merged into and both Ministry of Health post ids, so an old join key can be followed rather than only found missing. The record count falls from 695 to 670 and nothing was deleted: 588 records are bilingual, up from 563.

**The registry publishes each establishment twice and the pairing only caught 563 of them.** The Ministry of Health lists every facility once in French and once in Arabic under two post ids, and the generator pairs the two into one record by locality, then by an unambiguous shared commune. Where neither could decide, the record shipped in one language: 132 of them, 66 French-only and 66 Arabic-only, several of them obviously the same place as the record beside them. The registry's own habit is the missing signal, since it usually publishes the two posts under consecutive ids, but adjacency alone proves nothing: only 42.9% of French posts have an Arabic post at id+1, and in Merouana a single Arabic post sits next to both of the town's two hospitals.

So a candidate now needs the adjacent post id **and** a name check, and the name check compares consonant skeletons rather than transliterated strings. Arabic writes no vowels, so `translitAr` turns الشرفة into `chrfh`, which is two edits from "Chorfa" and read as a different place by the token similarity the pairing already had; on consonants the two agree. The same fold counts a doubled consonant once (Massika / مسيكة), drops the trailing h that ة surfaces as, and folds the g, q and k that ق romanizes as (Guettara / القطارة). A translation is not a transliteration, so the registry's own recurring adjectives are listed as equivalences, `ancien` with قديم and `nouvel` with جديد. The specialty code is read from the title rather than from the pairing's own field, which only records one for an EHS: the ophthalmology hospitals of Bechar and Djelfa are filed as EPH, one writes the Greek root and the other "medicine of the eyes", and the code is the only thing their two halves share. A pair whose names agree on nothing specific is refused; a pair where one side names nothing specific at all is accepted, because silence is not a contradiction, and that is what "EPH Tiaret" and المؤسسة العمومية الإستشفائية تيارت amount to. A post with two equally good candidates is left as two records rather than guessed at.

Twenty-five pairs were recovered and five adjacent candidates refused. Every decision, with the keys each side offered and the reason for each refusal, is in [`quality/sante-twin-recovery.json`](https://github.com/yasserstudio/geoalgeria/blob/main/quality/sante-twin-recovery.json), regenerated on every build so it cannot drift from the data.

**A record now cites both of its registry posts, and that is what pins its id.** `refs.msp_twin` is new: the other-language post for the same establishment, alongside the primary `refs.msp`. The carry-over key was the record's own primary post, which moved whenever the pairing did, so a paired record that lost its French post flipped to the Arabic id, retired its published id and minted a new one for a place that had not moved. The key is now resolved through either post, so gaining a twin, losing one, or merging two half-records all land on the id the place already had.

**Two Wikidata hospitals go back on the right establishment.** Both sat on the wrong one and were removed rather than corrected in the previous release, because each city carries several hospitals and the token matcher had nothing to tell them apart. `Q18785599`, hôpital d'Adrar, inception 1975, is the EPH of Adrar (`01-eph-01`), not the Nouveau Hopital; `Q7894776` is the CHU of Oran (`31-chu-01`), not the EHU, which its own labels say in both languages. Each was read from the Wikidata entity before being pinned, P31 instance-of hospital, P131 located-in Adrar and Oran, a P625 coordinate 1.6 km and 1.7 km from the commune centre, and a Wikipedia article as a second source. Both pins are keyed on the Ministry of Health post id, so they survive a re-pairing.

**The two Algiers facilities the owner located by hand publish at full precision.** `16-ehs-03`, the cardiac-surgery Clinique Mohamed Abderrahmani in Bir Mourad Rais, and `16-ehs-04`, the CNMS Dr Maouche Mohand Amokrane in Dely Ibrahim, each shipped as two half-records standing on one point, which the writer's shared-point rule has to demote to `approximate`. Merged, each is one record on its own point and publishes `exact`. The merged Maouche record reads الدكتور معوش محند أمقران, the Kabyle given name, decided by the project owner on 2 October 2026 and corroborated by the registry's own French post, which writes MOHAND; only its Arabic post writes محمد, and that spelling stays documented in `quality/overrides/sante.json`.

Also: the Setif CHU keeps `19-chu-01` and, merged, matches the hospital building `way/150640425` instead of an unnamed `amenity=clinic` node, so 113 records carry an OpenStreetMap point rather than 114 while three carry a Wikidata one rather than one. `node/4202804189` and `node/4077454962` are released back to the community tier. Geocoded records move from 600 to 587 of a smaller total, and 83 establishments remain unplaced, down from 93.
